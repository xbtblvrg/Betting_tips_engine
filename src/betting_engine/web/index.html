Stakes and Limits

Stress Value picks use $1–$5 stakes from stressed probability and odds. Model Picks can use $1–$5 stakes from nominal-model estimates, odds and related-game exposure; previously issued stakes remain unchanged. With all-tier sizing enabled, Probability Picks use a calculated non-Kelly base unit within $1–$5, reduced for related bets and capped by any supplied bankroll limits; their model-estimated return may be nonpositive. The $1 ticket minimum can offset the raw related-bet discount. Earlier sizing modes keep Probability Picks capped at $1. Without a bankroll, a bounded multiplier scales your base unit; related same-game bets reduce it. This unit policy is not personalized Kelly sizing. With a bankroll, fractional Kelly and bankroll limits apply. Leave the daily stake limit blank for no fixed daily cap. The $1–$5 per-bet range applies. Qualified additional contracts on the same game are allowed.
